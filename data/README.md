# Dataset records

Dataset selection and downloads are **not started**. Choose a licensed industrial surface-defect dataset with bounding-box annotations, or document and validate any necessary annotation conversion. Classification-only labels do not establish localization ground truth.

Raw/processed datasets belong in ignored locations such as `data/raw/` and `data/processed/`. Version only small manifests under `data/manifests/` and metadata under `data/metadata/`; do not place bulk data in those exceptions. Record provenance/license, annotation format, classes, content checksums, immutable split assignments, leakage safeguards, and external storage/retrieval details. No split exists yet. Follow SIRP 2.0 before any experiment.
